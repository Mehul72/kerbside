# Kerbside NSW privacy policy

_Last updated 21 August 2026._

Kerbside does not collect anything.

There is no account, no server, and no analytics. The app makes no network
requests at all and works fully in airplane mode.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything Kerbside remembers is written to your iPhone and stays there:

- Where you parked, as a latitude and longitude
- When you parked, and any note you added
- The text read from the parking sign you photographed
- Photographs you took or chose

This is held in the app's own storage on the device, shared only between
Kerbside and its own widgets. It is not backed up to us, because there is no
us to back it up to. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

## Location

Kerbside asks for your location only while you are using it, and only to record
where you left the car and to work out how far away it is now. The calculation
happens on the device. Your location is never transmitted.

You can refuse location access and still use the app: a saved spot will
remember the time, the sign and your note, but will not be able to point you
back.

## Photographs

Photographs are processed on the device using Apple's on-device Vision
framework to read the text on a parking sign. They are not uploaded.

## Other apps

Tapping "Walk me there" opens Apple Maps with your car's coordinates. At that
point Apple Maps takes over and its own privacy policy applies. Kerbside itself
still makes no network request.

## Notifications

If you turn reminders on, notifications are scheduled locally on the device by
iOS. There are no push notifications, so nothing is sent through a server.

## Children

Kerbside collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published here.

## Your choices and deletion

You can deny or revoke location, camera, photo-library, or notification access
at any time in iOS Settings. You can clear past spots inside Kerbside NSW.
Deleting the app deletes the active spot, history, saved photographs, and
preferences held in its app container.
