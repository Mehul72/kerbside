# Kerbside NSW support

Kerbside NSW remembers where you parked, reads NSW parking plates on your
iPhone, and counts down a limit you choose.

## Contact

For help, bug reports, or feedback, email
[mehulfursule@gmail.com](mailto:mehulfursule@gmail.com).

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the
problem occurred. Do not send a photograph if it contains private information.

## Before contacting support

- If a location was not saved, check **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location
  Services → Kerbside NSW** and allow access while using the app.
- If reminders do not arrive, check **Settings → Notifications → Kerbside NSW**.
- Sign reading is designed for New South Wales parking plates. An unreadable
  panel is shown as unread rather than guessed.

Kerbside NSW never decides whether you may park. The sign on the street remains
the only authority.
