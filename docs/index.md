# Kerbside NSW

Remember where you parked, what the NSW parking sign said, and how much time is
left. Kerbside NSW works on your iPhone without an account, server, analytics,
or advertising.

- Save the car's location and a private note or photograph.
- Read NSW parking plates on device.
- Count down a limit on the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and widgets.
- Receive local reminders before time runs out.

Kerbside NSW is a notebook, not a parking authority. Always read the sign on the
street before walking away.
